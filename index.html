Chapter One

The 3 AM Miracle

The soft, lonely blue light of my phone screen lit up my dark room. I was just scrolling aimlessly through social media, not looking for anything special, just trying to pass time.

Then, a quiet notification popped up on my screen.

It was a new message on LinkedIn. I opened it, expecting another dry, formal business text about work or network connections. But instead, my eyes met a warm, gentle message from a girl named Amara. A girl I had never seen or heard of in my entire life. A girl I had no single idea would soon become my whole world, my breath, and my ultimate ride-or-die.

Past 3 AM — The exact moment a simple LinkedIn message changed my entire life forever.

She simply sent a text checking up on me. It was so simple, yet so pure and sincere. There was an irresistible sweet energy around her from that very first second. Something inside me screamed not to ignore her. I decided to take her seriously right away.

"So, what kind of business model do you run?" she asked me.

She was so funny, so lively, and so full of sweet joy! Her words felt like warm morning sunlight breaking through a cold, dark room. Curious to know more about this incredible lady, I clicked on her LinkedIn profile. That was when I discovered she was an author! She had already written and published her very own book.

I got her book and read every single line with pure excitement. It was amazing. After reading it, I set up a call with her so we could share our thoughts. I gave her my honest, loving review and pointed out a few tiny things she could tweak to make it even better. We talked for hours that night. By the time I dropped my phone, a sweet, overwhelming realization hit my soul: I really, really like this girl.
